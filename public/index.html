GOOD QUESTIONS, WELCOME

A few things
you might wonder.

Not sure where to begin? Start here, or ask us directly.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
How do I enquire about a puppy?+

Use any enquiry link on this page. Tell us which breed interests you and ask about current availability and relevant details.

Which breeds are currently available?+

The breeds shown are examples only, not a live stock list. Please contact the business to confirm which breeds, if any, are currently available.

Can I ask about vaccination and health records?+

Absolutely. Ask what health records, vaccination and deworming documentation are available, and whether you can review them before deciding.

Can I arrange a visit?+

Contact the business first to discuss whether a visit can be arranged and confirm a suitable time. Please don't assume walk-ins are available.

What should I prepare before bringing a puppy home?+

Prepare a safe resting space, suitable food and water bowls, a comfortable bed, cleaning supplies, and a daily-care plan. Ask a qualified veterinarian for healthcare and feeding guidance.

Do you guide first-time pet parents?+

The business welcomes questions from new pet parents. Message to discuss the guidance available and responsibilities involved in caring for a puppy.
